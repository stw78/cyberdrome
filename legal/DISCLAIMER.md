## English

- **No warranty.** Cyberdrome is provided WITHOUT ANY WARRANTY, to the extent permitted by law. See sections 15–16 of
  the GPL.
- **A simulator for development and testing.** Cyberdrome is not meant for use while driving. Values, trouble codes and
  codings of the built-in car models are made-up examples, not manufacturer data.
- **Real cars at your own risk.** The program is read-only by default. Writing to control units must be enabled by you
  and confirmed for each write. Incorrect writes can disable a control unit or the vehicle. Do not use Cyberdrome to
  bypass security features or for modifications that break the law or type approval. No manufacturer security
  algorithms are included.
- **Google images and other downloads.** The Android emulator and system images (including Google APIs/Play images) are
  downloaded by you from Google under Google's terms. Play images are for development and testing only. Maps
  (© OpenStreetMap contributors), car database data (NHTSA vPIC, Wikidata, OBDb) and F-Droid apps are also downloaded
  by you under their own terms. None of it is part of Cyberdrome.
- **Privacy.** The program does not send information to other systems unless you ask for it. There is no telemetry.
  The GitHub update check (at most once a day) can be turned off under Settings → Updates → Advanced. Maps, the car
  database and Android downloads contact their sources only when you use them. See PRIVACY.md.
- **No logos.** Themes and skins are inspired by car brands but drawn from scratch. No manufacturer logos, fonts or
  graphics are included.
